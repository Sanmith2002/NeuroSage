# Feature 007 Research Decisions

## Decision summary

Feature 007 uses one protocol-approved empirical selective-risk ceiling and chooses the cutoff with maximum supported patient-level coverage on a distinct policy-selection cohort. It does not claim a finite-sample risk guarantee, independent final performance, clinical validity, or deployment readiness. One patient contributes one protocol-fixed index assessment per exact model/version/label scope. A single TRUST cutoff is selected; valid below-cutoff cases are CAUTION, and invalid/unavailable/incompatible/not-ready cases are DEFER.

The new policy cohort is the validation partition of a new, independently authorized Feature 003 build. It must be reserved for this purpose before correctness outcomes are inspected and patient-disjoint from estimator training/selection, calibrator fitting, and protected final-test populations. It becomes spent after policy selection. There is no such dataset or configured trust ledger now; without verified evidence and an approved protocol, operations are `NOT_READY`.

## Empirical method

For eligible index assessments `i` in one exact scope, let `q_i` be the private frozen estimator/calibrator score and `y_i=1` indicate verified primary-prediction correctness. The protocol defines selection membership `S_t(q_i)` for a candidate cutoff `t`.

For a nonempty selected set:

`R_emp(t) = Σ_i 1[S_t(q_i) and y_i=0] / Σ_i 1[S_t(q_i)]`.

For a nonempty eligible scope:

`C_emp(t) = Σ_i 1[S_t(q_i)] / N_eligible`.

Candidates must be deterministic and permitted by the approved protocol. The cutoff maximizes coverage subject to `R_emp(t)` meeting the protocol ceiling and any predeclared support rules. Score equality, tie handling, candidate generation, index assessment and eligibility are fixed before outcomes are examined. A zero-denominator risk is `undefined`; zero selected patients cannot produce a successful policy. Tied candidate cutoffs that induce the same selected set use the protocol's deterministic canonical tie rule.

These are empirical values from policy-selection evidence. They are subject to selection optimism and do not establish a risk bound, population generalization, clinical safety, or independent evaluation.

## Literature and applicability

1. **El-Yaniv, R., & Wiener, Y. (2010). “On the Foundations of Noise-free Selective Classification.” Journal of Machine Learning Research, 11(53), 1605–1641.** [JMLR article](https://jmlr.org/papers/v11/el-yaniv10a.html). Establishes risk–coverage as the central selective-classification trade-off. It supports the vocabulary and estimand, not a Safira threshold or clinical claim.
2. **Geifman, Y., & El-Yaniv, R. (2017). “Selective Classification for Deep Neural Networks.” Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 30, 4878–4887.** [NeurIPS paper](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Paper.pdf). Demonstrates post-hoc thresholding on a score with a user-specified risk target and a finite-sample procedure under that paper's assumptions. Safira does not adopt the reported guarantee: its approved V1 uses empirical selection risk, patient-level index observations, and no unapproved distributional or inferential assumption.
3. **Geifman, Y., & El-Yaniv, R. (2019). “SelectiveNet: A Deep Neural Network with an Integrated Reject Option.” Proceedings of the 36th International Conference on Machine Learning, PMLR 97, 2151–2159.** [PMLR article](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/geifman19a.html). Jointly optimizes predictor and reject mechanism; deferred because it changes/trains the primary predictor and conflicts with Safira's post-hoc boundary.
4. **Bates, S., Angelopoulos, A., Lei, L., Malik, J., & Jordan, M. (2021). “Distribution-Free, Risk-Controlling Prediction Sets.” arXiv:2101.02703.** [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.02703). Uses held-out data to control expected loss for prediction-set procedures. It is not adopted as Feature 007's reject/three-status policy algorithm; adapting its assumptions or guarantee requires separate research approval.

No published result is treated as clinical validation or automatic approval of a numerical operating point. The chosen empirical risk–coverage rule is a simple, auditable policy-selection criterion because it aligns with the approved research scope and does not assert a guarantee that the evidence/assumptions do not establish.

## Architecture findings from repository inspection

Inspected the committed Feature 001–006 contracts and source on the Feature 006 closure commit:

- **Feature 003** (`src/safira/dataset/records.py`, `specs/003-patient-grouped-safety-dataset/`) provides exact verified correctness examples and a manifest-bound ordinary `DatasetBuildResult`. Its normal roles are `train`, `validation`, `calibration`, and protected test. A distinct new dataset can reserve its own validation partition for policy selection without reusing the old Feature 005/006 partitions. The verifier must bind the complete inventory and check cross-dataset patient disjointness; ordinary results do not include protected rows.
- **Feature 004** (`src/safira/signals/service.py`, `src/safira/signals/reference.py`, `src/safira/signals/records.py`) creates deterministic reference states from training evidence and applies them to validation/calibration rows of that same build. `FittedStateReference` records original dataset/protocol/manifest identities and safe digests, not the raw fitted representation/category state. `SignalFeatureSet` does not export a reusable fitted-state object. `SignalService.build_feature_set` fits references on its input build's training partition; it cannot be pointed at an unrelated policy build while preserving the original state IDs.
- **Feature 005** (`src/safira/training/artifacts.py`, `src/safira/training/features.py`, `specs/005-automatic-model-specific-safety-training/contracts/training-python.md`) exports immutable exact-scope estimator parameters, frozen schema/preprocessing and original Feature 003/004 provenance. Its public contract offers train/artifact validation, not estimator inference.
- **Feature 006** (`src/safira/calibration/records.py`, `src/safira/calibration/verification.py`, `src/safira/calibration/scores.py`, `src/safira/calibration/service.py`) verifies the calibrator against its original base artifact/dataset/signal references and fits a mapping. `CalibrationService` has no application method. Its private `_reconstruct_scores` reconstructs Feature 005's base logistic linear predictor for the calibration fit; it does not apply the calibrator mapping to a new cohort. Its bundle verifier is purpose-specific and checks exact calibration partition alignment.

**Conclusion**: There is no supported public Features 004–006 API to score the new policy cohort. Feature 007 must implement a private compatibility bridge that admits original and new evidence separately, deterministically reconstructs the original Feature 004 reference state only from the original verified training partition, checks every reference digest, derives each policy signal row from the admitted Feature 002 prediction using the original `SignalProtocol` and existing Feature 004 record-level extraction semantics, applies only the reconstructed reference states for reference-based dimensions, and verifies exact one-to-one prediction/row alignment. It then reconstructs the exact frozen Feature 005 schema/preprocessing/estimator and applies the exact validated Feature 006 mapping. Caller-constructed policy rows are not accepted as trusted feature evidence. The bridge may not call Feature 006 calibration on policy data, refit any state on policy data, fabricate matching dataset references, or expose individual scores. This is feasible only when trusted origin/linkage verifiers and exact original training inputs are available. The bridge must be version-bound, fail closed on any mismatch, and tested against production synthetic Feature 004/005/006 outputs. Otherwise the scope remains `NOT_READY`.

## Risks and open implementation dependencies

- No production trust ledger, custodian, source verifier, or cross-source patient-linkage service is configured. They are environment dependencies, not built-in guarantees.
- Original Feature 003 training inputs, original Feature 004 protocol and deterministic fitted-state reconstruction inputs must remain available under trusted controls. If raw evidence is unavailable, the exact references cannot be reconstructed from Feature 004's current safe records alone.
- Feature 005/006 and Feature 004 internal numerical helpers are not exported public inference/application contracts. The Feature 007 bridge must use allowlisted artifacts and either stable documented pure transformations or a small versioned adapter, with synthetic numeric equivalence tests and no upstream modification.
- The independent policy-selection validation dataset and numeric protocol values do not currently exist. The public workflow must return `NOT_READY` until they do.
- Empirical maximum-coverage selection is optimistic on its selection cohort. A later independent evaluation is mandatory before any broader claim; this is separate from Feature 007.
- Patient-level index selection must be protocol- and scope-specific; protocol approval is required before outcome access. No universal index visit, risk ceiling, minimum group size, or support threshold is specified here.
